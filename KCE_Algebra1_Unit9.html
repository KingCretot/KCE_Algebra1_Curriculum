Unit 9

Absolute Value & Function Transformations

Four lessons covering Florida B.E.S.T. standards MA.912.AR.4.1, MA.912.AR.4.3, and MA.912.F.2.1. Absolute value equations and graphs come first, so the V-shaped graph can join lines and parabolas as a third family for the transformation lessons. MA.912.F.2.1 is split across two lessons: shifts (f(x) + k and f(x + k)), then stretches and reflections (kf(x) and f(kx)).
