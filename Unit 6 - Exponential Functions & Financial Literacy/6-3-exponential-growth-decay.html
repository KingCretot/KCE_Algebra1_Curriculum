Let's Begin

Adding versus multiplying

Option A: you get $1,000 a day for 30 days. Option B: you get 1 cent on day 1, and the amount doubles every day. Option A totals $30,000. Option B pays more than $5 million on day 30 alone. That's the difference between adding the same amount and multiplying by the same amount.

A quantity that multiplies by the same factor over each equal time step follows an exponential function. Today's job is to recognize whether it is growing or decaying — and by what percent — from its equation or a description.

By the end of today, you will be able to:

  • 1Identify the initial value and the factor in y = a(b)x
  • 2Classify an exponential function as growth or decay
  • 3Connect the factor b to a percent rate of change

Vocabulary

The two numbers that control everything

Exponential Function

A function of the form f(x) = a(b)x, where a ≠ 0, b > 0, and b ≠ 1. The variable is in the exponent.

💡Read it as: start at a, then multiply by b for each 1-unit step in x.
Growth and Decay Factor

b is the factor. If b > 1, the function shows growth. If 0 < b < 1, it shows decay.

💡Percent link: b = 1 + r for growth and b = 1 − r for decay, where r is the percent rate written as a decimal.

How It Works

Read a and b, then decide

1
Find the initial value a
It's the output when x = 0, since b0 = 1. In y = 500(1.04)x, a = 500.
2
Find the factor b
It's the base of the power. In y = 500(1.04)x, b = 1.04.
3
Compare b to 1
b > 1 → growth. 0 < b < 1 → decay.
4
Convert to a percent
Growth: r = b − 1. Decay: r = 1 − b. Here r = 0.04, so 4% growth per step.

💭 Notice: The initial value a doesn't decide growth or decay — only b does. y = 0.5(3)x starts small but grows fast.

Real-World Context

Growth and decay all around you

Populations, viral videos, and savings accounts can grow exponentially. Medicine leaving the bloodstream and a car losing value can decay exponentially. In every case, the amount changes by the same percent each time period, not the same number.

Example: A car worth $24,000 loses 15% of its value each year: V(t) = 24000(0.85)t. The factor 0.85 is less than 1, so this is decay — each year the car keeps 85% of the previous year's value.

Worked Example · From an Equation

Classifying f(x) = 3(1.25)x and g(x) = 80(0.6)x

Goal: Classify each function as growth or decay and give its percent rate.

1
The first function: f(x) = 3(1.25)x
a = 3 and b = 1.25. Since 1.25 > 1, this is growth. Rate: 1.25 − 1 = 0.25, or 25% per step.
2
The second function: g(x) = 80(0.6)x
a = 80 and b = 0.6. Since 0 < 0.6 < 1, this is decay. Rate: 1 − 0.6 = 0.4, or 40% per step.
3
Check g with numbers
g(0) = 80, g(1) = 48, g(2) = 28.8. Each value is 60% of the one before, so 40% is lost each step.
Growth: f(x) = 3(1.25)x
Decay: g(x) = 80(0.6)x

Worked Example · From a Description

Writing and classifying from words

Goal: A town of 12,000 people shrinks by 3% each year. Write the function and classify it.

1
Initial value
a = 12,000
2
Factor
Losing 3% keeps 97%: b = 1 − 0.03 = 0.97
3
Function and classification
P(t) = 12000(0.97)t. Since 0.97 < 1, this is decay.

💭 Notice: A common trap is writing b = 0.03 for a 3% decrease. That would mean losing 97% every year. The factor is what's kept, not what's lost.

Your Turn — Guided Practice

Now you try, with me right here

A bacteria culture starts with 200 cells and grows 15% every hour.

Question 1 — What is the growth factor b?

Here's why: Growing by 15% means keeping 100% and adding 15%: b = 1 + 0.15 = 1.15. A factor of 0.15 would shrink the culture to 15% of its size every hour.

Question 2 — Which function models the culture?

Here's why: The initial value goes in front and the factor is the base: C(t) = 200(1.15)t. Check: C(0) = 200 ✓ and C(1) = 230, which is 15% more.

Why This Matters

Spotting multiplication changes your predictions

Once you know a situation is exponential, you know its long-run behavior: growth eventually outruns any linear model, and decay keeps shrinking toward zero without quite reaching it. That changes how you plan, invest, and budget.

This shows up everywhere

  • Investing: money that grows by a percent each year.
  • Health: how long a medication stays at an effective level.
  • Ecology: tracking endangered populations or invasive species.

Mini Knowledge Test

Show what you know — 5 quick questions

Question 1
Is y = 7(0.92)x growth or decay?
Here's why: Only the factor b decides. Here b = 0.92, which is between 0 and 1, so the function decays — by 8% per step. The 7 is just the starting value.
Question 2
What percent rate does f(x) = 40(1.06)x represent?
Here's why: b = 1.06 is greater than 1, so it's growth. The rate is b − 1 = 0.06, or 6% per step.
Question 3
A phone loses 22% of its value each year. What is the decay factor?
Here's why: Losing 22% means keeping 78%: b = 1 − 0.22 = 0.78.
Question 4
A video has 500 views, and its views double every day. Which function models views after d days?
Here's why: Start at 500 and multiply by 2 each day: V(d) = 500(2)d. 500 + 2d adds just 2 views a day, which is linear.
Question 5
Which function shows decay?
Here's why: Decay needs a factor between 0 and 1. Only y = 900(0.5)x has b = 0.5. In the other choices the small number is the starting value, not the factor.
