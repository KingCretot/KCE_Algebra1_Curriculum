Key Ideas · Lesson 1.1

Reading the parts of an expression

Lesson 1.1

Every part has a job

A term is a piece separated by + or −. The coefficient is the number multiplying a variable, the constant is a number with no variable, and factors are things being multiplied. A group in parentheses can act as one single factor.

Example: In 4(x + 2) − 7 there are two terms, 4(x + 2) and −7. The factors of the first term are 4 and (x + 2). The constant is −7.
Lesson 1.1

Parts have meaning in context

In a real-world formula, the coefficient is usually a rate and the constant is usually a flat or starting amount.

Example: A phone plan costs C = 0.15m + 20. The 0.15 is the cost per minute; the 20 is the monthly fee you pay even with zero minutes.

Key Ideas · Lessons 1.2–1.3

Rearranging and solving

Lesson 1.2

Literal equations: treat other letters like numbers

To solve a formula for one letter, undo operations in reverse order using inverse operations. Every other letter just rides along.

Example: Solve A = lw for w: divide both sides by l, so w = A/l. Solve y = mx + b for x: subtract b, then divide by m, so x = (y − b)/m.
Lesson 1.3

Multi-step equations follow a fixed order

Distribute, combine like terms on each side, gather the variable terms on one side, then undo addition and subtraction before multiplication and division. Always check by substituting.

Example: 5(x − 2) = 3x + 6 → 5x − 10 = 3x + 6 → 2x − 10 = 6 → 2x = 16 → x = 8. Check: 5(6) = 30 and 3(8) + 6 = 30 ✓
Lesson 1.3

Word problems: translate first

Name the unknown, turn each phrase into a piece of the equation, then solve.

Example: A rental costs $30 plus $0.25 per mile, and the bill was $52: 30 + 0.25m = 52, so 0.25m = 22 and m = 88 miles.

Watch Out

The mistakes that cost the most points

The mistakeThe fix
Distributing to only the first term: 3(x + 4) → 3x + 4Multiply every term inside: 3(x + 4) = 3x + 12.
Dropping a negative when distributing: −2(x − 5) → −2x − 10−2 times −5 is +10, so the answer is −2x + 10.
Combining unlike terms: 4x + 3 → 7xOnly terms with the same variable and power combine. 4x + 3 stays as it is.
Dividing only part of a side: 2w = P − 2l → w = P − lDivide the whole side: w = (P − 2l)/2.
Skipping the checkSubstitute your answer into the original equation. If the two sides don't match, look for the slip.

Practice Test

10 questions, every lesson mixed together

Just like a unit test, these jump between lessons. You get two tries on each question, and every question explains itself after you answer.

Question 1
In 9 − 4x + 2x², what is the coefficient of x?
Here's why: The coefficient comes with its sign. The term is −4x, so the coefficient is −4. The 9 is the constant, and 2 belongs to the x² term.
Question 2
A taxi fare is F = 2.40m + 3.50, where m is miles. What does 3.50 represent?
Here's why: 3.50 has no variable attached, so it doesn't change with distance. It's the flat starting charge. The 2.40 is the per-mile rate.
Question 3
In 6(a + 5), what is being treated as a single factor?
Here's why: The parentheses group a + 5 into one quantity that is multiplied by 6. Until you distribute, the whole group acts as one factor.
Question 4
Solve A = lw for l.
Here's why: l is multiplied by w, so divide both sides by w: l = A/w. Check with numbers: a 12-square-foot rug that is 3 feet wide is 12 ÷ 3 = 4 feet long.
Question 5
Solve y = mx + b for x.
Here's why: Undo in reverse order: subtract b first (y − b = mx), then divide the whole side by m: x = (y − b)/m. The choice y/m − b divides only y instead of the whole side.
Question 6
A circle's circumference is C = 2πr. Which expression gives r?
Here's why: r is multiplied by 2π, so divide both sides by the entire 2π: r = C/(2π).
Question 7
Solve: 4(x − 3) = 20
Here's why: Distribute: 4x − 12 = 20. Add 12: 4x = 32. Divide by 4: x = 8. Check: 4(8 − 3) = 4(5) = 20 ✓. 5.75 forgets to multiply the 3 by 4, and 2 subtracts 3 instead of adding it.
Question 8
Solve: 7x + 5 = 3x − 11
Here's why: Subtract 3x from both sides: 4x + 5 = −11. Subtract 5: 4x = −16. Divide by 4: x = −4. Check: 7(−4) + 5 = −23 and 3(−4) − 11 = −23 ✓. −1.6 comes from adding 3x instead of subtracting it.
Question 9
A gym charges $25 to join plus $15 per month. Maria has paid $145 in total. Which equation finds the number of months, m?
Here's why: The $25 is paid once, so it stands alone. The $15 is paid every month, so it multiplies m. The total is 145: 25 + 15m = 145, which gives m = 8 months.
Question 10
Jaylen solves 2(x + 4) = 14 and gets x = 3. What should he do next?
Here's why: Checking takes seconds: 2(3 + 4) = 2(7) = 14 ✓. Substituting into the original equation is the quickest way to be sure no step slipped.
